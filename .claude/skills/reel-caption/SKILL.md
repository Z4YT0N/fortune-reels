# Reel caption

Adapted from the team's `fortune-code-caption` skill (Jake Schincariol's MIT ig-caption).

- Job A (the reel already hooked them): the caption gives context, search phrases and the ask; it
  is not a second hook. Job B (the caption is the content): line one is the hook.
- The feed shows ~125 characters before "... more". Count line one and show that window in a box.
  Never open with a greeting, a hashtag or an emoji.
- Shape: line 1 (<= 125 chars) / 2-6 short paragraphs with the search phrases written as people
  type them («تصميم مواقع»، «شركة برمجة»، «نظام ERP»، «متجر إلكتروني») / ONE ask = the same keyword
  as in the video / up to five specific hashtags on the last line (never #viral #fyp #explorepage).
- No link in the caption (not clickable): point to the bio (fortunecode.org, or fortunecode.org/try
  for موظفك الذكي) or deliver by DM.
- Light Gulf Arabic like the reel. Brand vocabulary as in `rules/script.md`. Emoji sparingly.
- Remove em dashes and invisible Unicode.

Output: the visible-window box; the caption as ONE fenced text block; an optional first comment;
a receipt (job, visible chars, ask, hashtags count, search phrases placed). Nothing is posted.

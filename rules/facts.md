# Facts and sources

- Every number, study or story in a reel is verified on a source page that was actually opened
  (WebFetch or the browser), in this session or recorded in `memory/FACTS_BANK.md` with its URL.
- Prefer the primary source (the study, the court record, the company's own page). A blog quoting
  a number with no link to its origin is not a source.
- If a needed number cannot be verified, the script carries `{{رقم}}` and a flag. Never estimate,
  round up for drama or "remember" a number.
- Name the source in general terms on screen and in the VO («قوقل»، «دراسة مع ديلويت»، «متجر
  أمريكي كبير»); give the exact URLs to the owner under the script.
- Rejected claims go in FACTS_BANK too, with why, so nobody tries them again
  (example: "Amazon loses 1% of sales per 100 ms": no primary source found).
- Client names, logos and results only with written approval. Never imply a Saudi commercial
  registration.

Editorial poster plate, composition centred slightly low. In the middle of the frame: a messy pile of five framed black-and-white gallery photographs printed as coarse halftone riso cut-outs, overlapping at angles (a desert dune, a falcon on a perch, a mosque arcade, a carved wooden door, a dallah coffee pot). A huge heavy old cast-iron kettlebell-style weight has slammed down onto the centre of the pile: the glass of the frame under it is shattered into spider-web cracks and small shards jump out, one frame corner is bent upward. The cast-iron weight is the ONLY object printed in signal green #00DB33 riso ink with deep green #006A39 halftone shadows. Behind the whole pile sits a torn sheet of deep green #006A39 riso paper, mis-registered a few millimetres down and to the left, like the B reference. Every photograph and frame is black, grey and paper. One curious detail: a single small black-and-white bird feather floats in the air above and to the left of the weight, untouched. Torn paper edges, three strips of beige masking tape. The top 35 percent of the frame and the bottom 12 percent are calm empty warm off-white paper #F0F1F2 with only paper fibres and grain. Heavy film grain and print noise. No marker drawings, no arrows, no circles, no cursor, no text.

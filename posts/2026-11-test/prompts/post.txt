Square-ish editorial poster plate. In the lower two thirds: a messy pile of five framed black-and-white gallery photographs lying flat and overlapping on paper (a desert dune, a falcon on a perch, a mosque interior with arches, a carved wooden door, a dallah coffee pot). A huge heavy old cast-iron weight has slammed down onto the centre of the pile: the glass of the frames under it is cracked into spider-web shards and the frames are bent. The cast-iron weight is the ONLY object printed in signal green #00DB33 riso ink with deep green #006A39 shadows and a slightly mis-registered green offset; every photograph and frame is black, grey and paper. One curious detail: a single small black-and-white bird feather floats in the air just above the weight, top left of it. Cut-out collage edges, torn paper, two strips of beige masking tape at the corners of the pile. The entire top 40 percent of the frame is calm empty warm off-white paper #F0F1F2 with only paper fibres and grain. No marker drawings, no arrows, no circles, no cursor, no text.

## Measured text-motion observations (12 fps strips)
- **ammar (14):** Arabic typewriter grows ONE string, so the joins stay intact. The first words fill the
  frame, then the block pulls back in about 3 frames (0.25 s). Line 2 types in the accent colour at about 1.3 s for 18 chars.
- **ROAR (05):** a word arrives with about 2 frames of horizontal smear. A kashida stroke draws out. A paper
  note drops with a rotation in about 5 frames and an overshoot. 1-frame invert flash on a beat.
- **kayn (08):** kicker line in small grey, punch line word by word; each word grows lighter → full in about 3-4 frames.
- **rit2raj (09):** type-on per Latin char, about 1 char per frame. Big condensed keyword beside tiny italic.
- **getripping (00):** a new word drops from below the baseline. A highlight box wipes in behind the
  keyword («trial reels»). The bar chart keeps moving under the text.
- **jordans (16):** see 03. Scribble → type in 1-2 frames with a yellow flash, dashed guides, a selection
  box, typed words grey → ink, hand-drawn circle around «you're», LOVE letters gathering with orbit strokes.
- **koral (17):** typing in a chat box, each new word orange then dark, the box tilts as it grows, the cursor
  clicks send and the camera dives.

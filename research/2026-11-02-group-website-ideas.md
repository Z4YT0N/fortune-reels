# Group and holding website reels: research and 10 ideas (2026-11-02)

Audience: decision makers at Gulf groups and holding companies. Formula borrowed, never the video. Nothing here is verified as a number for use in a script. All 10 are concepts awaiting the owner's choice.

## Sample and limits

- 30 public web design videos from TikTok discover pages (`website-redesign-before-and-after`, `small-business-website-designer`, `company-website-design`, and five related slugs). Discover pages favour videos that already did well.
- The number used is the play count shown on discover cards. Likes were read on 9 video pages.
- Profile grids did not load without a login, so the median of the last 12 videos is unavailable. The outlier multiple cannot be computed, except a rough read on Elypt from 5 videos.
- Instagram was not read. Douyin was not reachable and web search found no matching Chinese account, so this sample has no Chinese creators.
- Hooks were read from captions plus still frames around second 7. First-frame motion was not observed. Audio was not heard.

Confidence: weak to medium. Good enough to pick tests. Not proof of what brings buyers.

## Findings

1. Before and after is the most repeated formula: 14 of 30, and 4 of the top 10 by plays. Four of the 14 are one account baiting a prompt giveaway, likely aimed at builders.
2. The strongest caption angle is perception or loss, not beauty: «different perception», «losing you patients».
3. Comment or DM keywords appear on the sales-minded videos (DENTAL, UPGRADE).
4. Same format, same account: Elypt ranged from 2,280 to 77.8K. Format alone does not explain the result. The winner had a rich visual and a tagged business. That is one data point.
5. Plays do not show buyers. Saves and likes may come from designers. Measure company chats.

## Owner rules applied

Gulf Arabic hook of 8 words or fewer. Frame 1 moves. Designer-made or real portfolio design only, never quick HTML. No client names or logos without written approval. No real company redesigned by name in the Gulf: fictional groups or anonymised work. No invented numbers. The CTA keyword «انطباع» is already approved.

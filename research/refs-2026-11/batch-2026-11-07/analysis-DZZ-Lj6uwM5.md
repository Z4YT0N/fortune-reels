## 1. Summary
**Layouts:**
- **SPLIT (72 %):** graphic card on top (~62 %), 1-3 word caption at y ≈ 1280, rounded talking-head card at y 1400-1920 (radius ≈ 40) with the head breaking out above it.
- **FULL (28 %):** 11 punches (0.8-6.8 s, average ≈ 3.4 s), each carrying a section takeaway or an aside.

**The framework:**
- **01 Context:** establish it immediately, through audio + visuals + text, ideally all three.
- **02 Lean:** make the viewer curious in one direction: proof/stat, pain/benefit, or something shocking.
- **03 Contrarian Snapback:** pull them away from the direction they were leaning.

**Story devices:**
- A recurring chapter "menu" card where the next bar sharpens on each return: a progress bar.
- Every claim gets a diagram: an audience dot circle, a skip-rate graph, a fishing-hook animation, a branching story arc, real example reels inside phones.
- The video uses its own method: title + script page with green HOOK lines inside 1.5 s (context), "scientifically impossible to skip" (lean), and the snapback saved for step 3.

**CTA:** "Comment 'hooks'". Backed by proof: a YouTube-style card whose view counter ticks 105K → 700K, "700,000 people… best hook training on the internet".

## 6. Rebuild in our stack
Arabic rule: one `<span class="w">` per whole word inside a right-to-left container; stagger right to left; never split letters.

1. **Split layout:**
   - the talking-head video inside a div with `border-radius: 40px`;
   - head pop-out = a second copy of the same video masked to the head (RVM/rembg; one Gemini cut-out still works on a locked camera);
   - backgrounds #121416 / #F0F1F2.
2. **Micro captions:** ElevenLabs word times, 1-2 Arabic words, `visibility` toggled on the frame with no tween, Zain Bold ~64 px.
3. **Chapter card:** `from('.title .w',{filter:'blur(12px)',opacity:0,duration:.16,stagger:.08})` → `to('.bar.active',{filter:'blur(0)'})` → a shine sweeping right to left. Arabic «طريقة الهوك الثلاثي», «٠١ السياق / ٠٢ الميلان / ٠٣ الارتداد المعاكس», active edge #00DB33. Chrome texture and 3D hook prop via Gemini green screen.
4. **Word-by-word blur-in titles,** 2-4 f per word.
5. **Title riding a circle:** MotionPath with WHOLE words (`autoRotate`), then cross-fade to flat. No SVG `textPath` for Arabic.
6. **Dot-field audience:** ring via `stroke-dasharray` (~24 f); dots `stagger:{each:.015, from:'random'}` on the spoken word; green #00DB33 = target clients, grey #6B7075 = others.
7. **Device proof:** a feed swipe inside the phone (`y: -100 %`, 6 f, power3.inOut); a 2×2 grid where the losers blur out. Real client screens only.
8. **Pen annotations:** SVG dashoffset over 6-8 f, two offset paths for pressure; green = right, red only for a mistake.
9. **Self-drawing graph:** card 4 f / axes 8 f / curves 80 f, glow via drop-shadow. Slow vs fast site, Excel vs system; verified numbers only.
10. **Script/sitemap sheet:** lines blink green (`tl.set` on frames 25/26/27), fill with a stagger, or whip aside.
11. **Whip slide:** 6 f with SVG `feGaussianBlur stdDeviation="20 0"` animated 0 → 20 → 0. The new card enters from the LEFT, following reading direction.
12. **Two background modes:** concept #121416, evidence #F0F1F2; a ~6 f background tween that also flips the caption colour.
13. **Sonar pulse:** radial rings scale 0.6 → 1.4 while fading 1 → 0 over ~10 f, in green.
14. **Physical analogy:** a falcon diving and banking, or a dhow turning (HyperFrames SVG with MotionPath, or a 4 s Flow clip).
15. **Branching arc:** a dot follows a dashed path, the branch draws, the diagram scales up while the example blurs.
16. **Formula cascade (signature move):** `tl.to('.bar',{backgroundColor:'#00DB33',color:'#121416',stagger:.12})` + one SFX hit on the first bar.
17. **Accent word:** Arabic has no italic, so use a weight/size contrast or a green box behind the key word.
18. **Nested proof:** our best result inside the opening phone.
19. **Example audio bleed:** 1-2 s at about −14 dB under the voice.
20. **Live counter:** GSAP number tween with `snap`; the number span stays left-to-right.
21. **Keyword CTA:** «اكتب "نظام"» with the keyword at 2x in green Zain Black, the DM automation wired to the same word.
22. **Sound:** a constant low bed at about −24 LUFS + ~1 hit per 9 s on graphic events (`adelay` + `amix`).

What the finished reels look like (`crops.jpg`):
- **Ground:** white, or warm beige paper with soft texture, and a moving gobo light (window or leaf shadows) sliding across it.
- **One hero object:** a realistic cut-out with a soft shadow (brain, skull, skeleton hand, key, mountain, chess piece). It turns slowly or lands with a small bounce.
  - Sometimes a person's black-and-white photo sits on a solid blue square card.
  - Sometimes the object sits in a circle with a dashed ring rotating around it.
- **Type mixes two voices:** a heavy condensed sans for the stress word, plus a thin italic serif connector («You don't have to *Move* mount everest», «Just KEEP That tiny COMMITMENT»).
  - A huge faded outline word sits behind the object; the bold word gets a black box.
- **Real props at the frame edges** (a pen, a razor blade) add depth.
- **Motion:** slow, smooth and minimal. One element moves at a time; the text writes on or slides with a stroke.
- **Lesson:** "premium minimal" = ONE object + mixed-voice type + soft moving light. Calm is the hook.

What the frames show (`sheet-QLYz2J6dac0.jpg`, `sheet-mcXr_DgwtQk.jpg`):
- **Planimetric, dead-centre framing:** the subject faces the camera square-on, the background is a flat wall or facade parallel to the lens, and the horizon sits exactly mid-frame.
- **Deadpan:** characters hold still and stare into the lens; the comedy comes from timing.
- **Locked-off camera.** The few moves are straight dollies or a whip pan; cuts happen on action.
- **Top-down tabletop inserts:** a cup on marble, hands entering to place a note or the keys.
- **Title cards in a bold geometric sans in yellow** («WALKING THE DOG IN TOWN 7:30PM»), centred, appearing in steps.
- **Palette:** pastel, warm golden hour, saturated but soft, with repeated symmetric props (lamps, mirrors).
- **Rhythm:** the same framing repeated with small changes between shots (a character, then the dog, then both).

## 1. شرح الهوك في صناعة المحتوى (ما هو الهوك في الانستاغرام و التيك توك)
Channel: Moulay Halim · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4lNxaQlM55A · 15,838 views · Arabic (Gulf-leaning
dialect: شنو، تبغى، يلي) · 5 min · 2025-03 · full transcript
- Defines the hook as the first 3-5 seconds, where the viewer decides to stay or swipe. Says strong value
  with a weak hook still fails.
- Claims to have studied thousands of winning videos and found 6 hook families. He teaches 3 (the other 3
  sit behind his paid academy).
- **Hook 1, «هوك العرّافة» (the fortune-teller / future hook).** Three steps: (1) name a problem in your field,
  (2) tie it to the viewer's future, (3) a question or line that makes them curious about that future.
  His 600k-view example: puts the viewer in their current situation, provokes them a little ("you've seen
  this technique but don't know its name, so you can't search for it or learn it"), then promises what
  they get if they keep watching.
- **Hook 2, «هوك التجربة» (the experiment hook).** Three steps: (1) a weakness or problem the audience has,
  (2) link it to an experiment you ran and the fix you found, (3) say why your fix differs from the others.
  His 500k-view example is a snapback list: «لا تنشر يوم الأحد، انشر يوم الاثنين… كل هذه الأشياء غلط»,
  i.e. name the common rules, then declare them all wrong, and finish with the fix and a CTA.
- **Hook 3, «الهوك الساحر» (the magician / visual hook).** 100% visual: an unexpected movement, a visual
  trick or a striking effect. Two steps: show a strange shot that makes the viewer ask "what's happening?",
  then **link that shot to the video's topic**. He notes big brands now use it in ads.
- Every hook type rests on a visual hook, an audio hook, or both together. Plan both layers.
- Close: soft CTA to a waitlist + subscribe. Total ~5 min, so it shows how a teaching video sells the
  next step without a hard pitch.

## 2. كيف نصنع محتوى ريلز مشوق وجذاب
Channel: الأكاديمية للتدريب الإعلامي · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uyt_zva7Yr4 · 531 views · Arabic (MSA +
Levantine) · 4 min · 2025-01 · full transcript (weak source: low views, generic advice)
- Reels run 15 s to 1 min. «خير الكلام ما قلّ ودلّ»: a fixed small number of short, direct sentences, because
  people scroll fast and any padding loses them.
- Open with a striking sentence or a strange question. Their example: «هل فكرت يوماً بأن تسكن في مقبرة؟».
  The odd question carries curiosity to the end.
- End by inviting a reply: «شاركنا رأيك» / «وأنت شو بتقول؟», so the viewer feels their opinion matters.
- **One picture per sentence.** Every line of text gets its own image or clip, cut fast to match the text.
- Many reels have no voice-over; then the music must carry the tone (news, human or light).
- Fonts and colours must be readable at scroll speed.
- Distribution advice (keywords in the caption, trending hashtags, post after 7 pm) is generic and
  partly contradicted by video 1 and by video 4. Do not adopt the fixed time.

## 4. What's Actually Working on Instagram in 2026 (Yapping, Trials & Reposts)
Channel: Think Media Podcast (guest: Brock Johnson, Build Your Tribe; says he interviewed Mosseri) ·
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=irUkFlX_dbE · 168,434 views · English · 55 min · 2026-06 · full transcript
- **Dead format:** a clickbait text hook on 7 s of trending audio plus "read the caption". It worked by
  faking watch time through loops. He says it no longer works.
- **Working formats:** raw "yap" talking clips (easy to prepare, hard to deliver: about 47 s with no filler
  and no tangents); new visual ways of saying the same idea; **series and challenges** (for example,
  "reviewing a reel every day until 1M"); street interviews. Changing the format alone can give 10-100x views.
- **1.7 seconds:** cites a Meta report saying people decide to scroll in about 1.7 s on average. The
  **visual** of the opening matters more than the words. Example: average reel skip rate 77%, one of his
  reels at 50% skip rate performed better.
- **Mosseri (as he relays it): likes drive reach to followers; shares drive reach to non-followers.**
  Shares are "number one" for virality. Comments and saves are "far far lower". Instagram's real goal
  is **DM conversations**: content that gets sent and starts chats.
- **SHARE acronym** for shareable reels: S = simple (a "drunk grandma" can follow it, even in the hook;
  avoid idioms and abstract analogies), H = hook, A = authentic (every layer of scripting, editing and
  reshooting strips emotion; his "good edit" post was his worst in 90 days), R and E = make it relatable
  and entertaining while educating, and **get specific** in captions, stories and on-screen labels.
- **Trial Reels:** shown only to non-followers. "Unlimited at-bats" to reach new people. The limit varies
  by account: **some get 5 a day, some up to 25 a day**. They can now be scheduled in the app and in
  Metricool. Use them for A/B: two hooks, or three edits of the same video.
- **Trap: graduating a trial does NOT put it on followers' feeds as a new post.** If a trial goes viral
  Thursday and you flip it Friday, followers don't get it as fresh content. His fix: keep a duplicate
  before posting, and post the winner as a **new, original feed reel**.
- **Duplicate rule:** trial first then feed = OK. Feed first then trial = flagged as duplicate. Instagram
  tightened this after marketers posted the same trial hundreds of times.
- **Repost your own reels 60-90 days later** (he reposts every reel, schedules the repost right away;
  says Mosseri confirmed it is allowed). Give each reel a second carousel slide or thumbnail, because
  Instagram can resurface the second slide. The "Repost" button on your own content does nothing.
- Frequency: 2-4 a day is fine; some accounts post 100+ a day and grow fastest. Volume beats follower
  count. A member made a $35k launch with 957 followers.
- Live + comment-keyword DM automation ("comment LIVE and I'll send the ticket") works only with an
  existing audience.

## 5. Copy This Strategy To Go Viral on Instagram Reels Every Time You Post in 2026 (new algorithm)
Channel: Robert Benjamin · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=77bcE3jrJR4 · 6,888 views · English · 11 min ·
2026-05 · full transcript (part of it is a sponsored tool pitch; ignored)
- **Text-only reels** (B-roll + on-screen text, no talking) travel across languages because Instagram
  translates text more easily than voice. Examples at 20k-168k views.
- Those reels pair a long caption with a **ManyChat comment-keyword** loop: viewers rewatch, comment the
  keyword, and the DM goes out. That counts as both reach and lead capture.
- **The cover is now a search asset.** Instagram's Explore grid, Instagram search and Google "short videos"
  all show covers. Put 3-6 curiosity words on every cover and choose it yourself (examples: "dumbest
  wedge ever", "cancer has a smell").
- **Account classification check:** niche keyword in the name field and in the bio, suggested-account
  setting turned ON, and the "similar accounts" list under your profile should show your niche. If it
  doesn't, Instagram has classified you wrong.
- **Metadata alignment:** the cover text, on-screen hook, spoken hook, caption, comment keyword and
  hashtags (max 5) must all say the same topic. Example: a diet reel uses the keyword "PLAN", which
  matches "diet plan" in the caption and on screen.
- Find "breakout" posts (2x a competitor's average views) and remake the format, not the words.
- Post when your audience is online (from Insights or competitor data).

## 6. Instagram Trial Reels: What They Are and How to Use Them in Your Instagram Strategy
Channel: Metricool · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3PxIS06cw8U · 23,909 views · English · 5 min · 2026-01
· full transcript
- Trial reels reach only non-followers and do not stay on the grid, so you can experiment without fear.
- What to test: (1) the same video with 2-3 hooks; (2) a different closing/CTA to see which drives
  interaction or profile visits; (3) the same topic from different angles (educational vs storytelling vs
  inspirational); (4) topics or niches you never dared post.
- **Trial as an introduction card:** since only strangers see it, make an intro reel whose only goal is
  "follow me / visit the profile".
- Auto-share to feed on good performance is a toggle at publish time (in the app and in Metricool).

## What this adds to, or contradicts in, our knowledge files

**Contradictions or corrections**
1. **Graduation ≠ a new post** (`02-trial-reels-and-growth.md` says graduation "sends it to followers").
   Per video 4, a flipped trial does not reach followers as fresh content. Our flow "winner graduates
   (MANUAL)" should become: **keep the master file, and post the winner as a new feed reel**. Optionally
   also flip the trial (Mosseri via Kontentino: harmless).
2. **Never trial something already on the feed.** It gets flagged as a duplicate. Order is always trial → feed.
   This is missing from 02.
3. **Trial cap varies by account (5 to 25 a day)**. 02 calls "20 a day" unofficial, which is right, but our
   real cap must be read from our own account, not assumed.
4. **What we measure.** 02 compares "views, retention and completion". Mosseri says to judge on **sends
   (shares), likes and watch time**, and compare trials only with trials. Shares per reach should be the
   main graduation metric for reach content.
5. **Polish vs authenticity.** `10-script-patterns.md` builds on fully produced ROAR-style reels. Video 4
   says heavy editing can lower results and raw talking works in 2026. This is not fatal (ROAR is
   produced and works in our market), but the trial lab should include a raw lane to test against it.
6. **Text-led formats.** Video 4 says "text hook + read the caption" is dead. Video 5 says text-only
   B-roll reels travel well. Both can be true: text-only is fine when the reel itself delivers the value;
   pointing to the caption is the dead part. Our xx call-and-response format (text-led, value on screen)
   is on the safe side.
7. Hook window: our pattern files use "first 2-3 s". Meta's figure (relayed) is **1.7 s**, and it is visual.
   The first frame must already carry the hook picture. A voice starting at 0.1-0.3 s (ROAR) is consistent
   with that.

**New, not in our files**
- Arabic hook taxonomy: العرّافة / التجربة / الساحر (video 1).
- Cover-as-search-asset, account classification check, and metadata alignment (video 5).
- Reposting every reel after 60-90 days (video 4).
- Series and challenge formats as the most reliable viral vehicle in 2026 (video 4).
- Trial as an "introduction card" whose only goal is the follow (video 6).

## Rules to adopt (25)

### Script
1. **Win the first 1.7 s visually.** The first frame must already show the hook picture: no logo or
   intro, and the text plus image already moving. Words support the picture; they don't replace it.
2. **Pick a hook family on purpose** and label it in the script metadata. Add these to our ROAR hooks:
   - العرّافة (future): problem → the owner's future → curiosity. «لو موقعك ما ينفتح من الجوال، بعد سنة وش
     بيصير لمبيعاتك؟»
   - التجربة (experiment): «جربنا X على مشروع حقيقي… والنتيجة غير اللي يقولونه» (real numbers from the facts
     file only).
   - الساحر (visual): a strange shot first (a screen falling apart, a site loading forever), then tie it to
     the service within 2 s.
3. **Snapback list hook:** list the habits everyone follows, then «كل هذا غلط»
   (video 1's 500k format). It matches ROAR's «لا تدور على…» and works well for myth-vs-reality reels.
4. **Simple enough for a "drunk grandma":** no idioms or abstract analogies in the hook; one concrete
   scene instead.
5. **Specific beats generic** in the hook, the on-screen labels and the caption: name the sector, the city,
   the number and the tool (Salla, ZATCA, «طلب من الجوال»).
6. **One picture per sentence** (video 2, and ROAR's 1-2 s per noun): the script is written as numbered
   lines, and each line maps to one visual.
7. **Text-only variants must deliver the value on screen.** Never "read the caption" as the payoff.
8. **The cover is part of the script:** write 3-6 curiosity words for the cover with every script, and
   never put the full answer on it.
9. **Keep a raw lane:** at least 1 in 5 trial variants is a raw talking or screen-recording version of a
   produced script, to test polish against authenticity in our market.

### CTA & funnel
10. **One CTA type per reel goal.** Reach reels end in a share prompt («أرسلها لصاحب مشروع يحتاجها»), because
    sends drive non-follower reach. Lead reels end in a comment keyword or WhatsApp.
11. **Comment-keyword CTA = keyword in the topic's language.** It must match the cover, hook, caption and
    hashtags (for example «موقع», «متجر», «نظام»). The DM bot then sends the asset and asks one qualifying
    question.
12. **Keep ROAR's qualifier in front of the action:** «إذا عندك متجر يبيع فعلاً، اكتب "متجر" في التعليقات».
    Qualifier + keyword + outcome.
13. **DM conversations are the conversion point, not followers.** 2k-follower accounts closed big deals in
    DMs (video 7). Measure leads per 1,000 reach, not follower growth.
14. **Metadata alignment checklist on every post:** cover text = on-screen hook = spoken hook = caption
    keyword = comment keyword = hashtags (≤5).
15. **No fake engagement hacks** (copy-link spam, comment pods). Shares must be real.

### Instagram growth & Trial Reels
16. **Graduate by reposting, not only by flipping.** Keep the master render; post the winner as a NEW feed
    reel (flipping the trial too is harmless).
17. **Order is trial → feed, never feed → trial** (duplicate filter).
18. **Graduation metric = shares (sends) per reach first,** then watch time and completion, then likes.
    Compare trials only with other trials.
19. **Read our own daily trial cap** in the app (5-25 a day varies by account) before sizing the lab;
    start at 3 a day as planned.
20. **Test more than hooks:** hook ×2-3, closing/CTA ×2, angle (educational / story / proof), and a new
    topic per batch.
21. **Make an intro-card trial** («مين حنا وليش تتابعنا») whose only goal is a follow or profile visit.
22. **Series and challenges** are the 2026 viral vehicle: for example «نبني موقع لمشروع سعودي كل أسبوع» or
    «نراجع موقع متابع كل يوم». Plan at least one running series.
23. **Repost every feed reel 60-90 days later,** with a different cover (and a second carousel slide where
    the format allows).
24. **Account classification:** service keyword in the name field («برمجة مواقع وتطبيقات»), in the bio and in
    the alt text; suggested-accounts setting ON; check that "similar accounts" shows our niche.
25. **Volume beats followers:** a steady 1-3 posts a day (feed + trials) beats occasional perfect reels.
    This fits our diagnosis that leads dropped when posting stopped.

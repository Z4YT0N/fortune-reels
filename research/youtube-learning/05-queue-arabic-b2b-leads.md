Channel: Arbaaa Marketing (أربعة) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_dzfektDNF4 · 104,504 views · Saudi Gulf

- Weak on tactics but useful for **real Saudi business register**: «وش قصدي في هذا الشيء؟», «مرة كبير»,

- Competitor study: take the best point from each competitor and fold it into your own content.

### 2. كيف تسوق خدماتك كمحترف؟
Channel: الدكتور ثابت حجازي · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zoZow0cwoyY · 161,079 views · Saudi/Hijazi
Gulf Arabic · 19.2 min · 2021-04 (a long Snapchat talk, dated) · 19.2 min transcribed
- **Audience call-out opening:** he lists the professions he is talking to («مصمم داخلي، مصمم جرافيك، مونتير،
  مصممة أزياء، مدرب…»), then «لازم يتابعني الآن». Naming the buyer's role in line 1 is a Gulf-native hook.
- **His website-programmer example is about us.** A developer asked "why $2,000?" answers «بـPHP» and

  off the client's plate. Lesson: **every price needs a ready persuasion script.** He says his team has

- **Guarantee a number the client already tracks:** a social-media designer failed with «جرب وحتشوف».
  The winning version he gives: "your last post got 180 shares; I guarantee my weakest post gets 300".
  So promise a measurable result in the client's own metric, not "quality".
- Services beat goods for marketing: a service can prove it is different (person, method, results), so it
  has more persuasion angles. Use them.
- For a service seller, **social media is the trust engine** (people check how you think and work before
  buying), and a **personal website** is the "official" proof step behind the bio link.
- Build the portfolio early: do a $50 project as if it were a $5,000 one, because it sells the next ones.
  He started at $200 a day as a trainer; now he charges $4-5k a day.

  and 10x prices. He says putting the company brand ahead of his own personal brand cost him three years.
- Saudi platforms (2021): X/Twitter, Snapchat, Instagram, YouTube. Pick 1-3 where your buyer is.
- Also: grow personal relationships. Every new contact in your field is a potential lead.

### 3. الهوك! السر يلي رح يجبلك ملايين المشاهدات
Channel: يوتيوب سكول (Youtube Skool) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bGNYKIlpdKQ · 36,499 views · Levantine

  2. **Shock statistic:** «٩٠٪ من قنوات اليوتيوب بتفشل بأول ٦ شهور».

- CTA: a free workshop link in the description (soft lead magnet).

### 4. لو بدأت صناعة محتوى في ٢٠٢٦ دي الحاجات اللي هعملها
Channel: Wael El-Selouky · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rINrLKwrT9c · 234,501 views · Egyptian Arabic ·
14.3 min · 2026-05 · 14.3 min transcribed
- **Hook = a statistic from a scene he witnessed:** at an event, about 90% of the audience raised their hands
  to "want to be a creator", and only 5% kept them up when asked who actually posted. A number you saw
  happen beats a quoted number.
- **«مليون متابع ولا مليون دولار؟»** Pick one. Followers do not turn into money by themselves; set the goal
  as sales, and stop judging success by views.
- **Have the offer ready before posting.** He says his viral years (2020-2023) sold nothing because nothing
  was in the bio; sales started only when reach had fallen. So put the booking link, landing page or
  product in the bio first, then post.
- **Three content types (funnel):**
  - Awareness: viral or trend content. It does not sell, and does not even need to teach. The only goal is
    to be seen.
  - Consideration: free value (a useful tip, a free file, a free course) that builds trust.
  - Conversion: a pure selling video, aimed at the people who took the free value.
  His live example: a new FreelanceX page runs viral reels → a free course reel → a "join the platform" video.
- **Market research, not invention:** copy what works from competitors (ideas that got views), never the
  words. Say it in your own way.
- Objections answered: nobody is watching you fail (50 views = nobody saw it); start with the phone, no gear.
- Lead magnet at the end: email → a file with the steps to post your first 10 videos.

### 5. ازاي تعمل خطة محتوى شهر كامل | خلاصة شغل مع أكثر من 100 عميل
Channel: Ahmed El-Sebaey (أحمد السباعي) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KJPf_mJGEDg · 41,467 views ·
Egyptian Arabic · 12.6 min · 2025-09 · 12.6 min transcribed
- **Fake-stakes hook he admits later:** "I'm late delivering a client's monthly plan…", then halfway he admits

- Research stack: list competitors → each one's all-time popular videos → their **trending in the last 24 h**

- Split ideas into **evergreen** (searched steadily) and **trendy** (only if it ties back to your core

  hub». HubSpot is his example.
- Monthly plan = 4 weekly themes. Each week: 1-2 long videos + 2-3 shorts (+ a community post), from the
  strongest researched ideas.
- **Link videos into series** so each one sends viewers to the next.
- Personality is the moat: people follow the person (his example: Abu Zaid stirring tea). Let the creator
  keep his own voice.

## B2B / agency leads

### 6. Watch This To Generate 1000s of Leads (In Any Niche)
Channel: Alex Hormozi · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mst4hreQYl0 · 893,350 views · English · 31.5 min ·
2025-11 · first 25.0 min transcribed
- **"Book a call / get a quote" only works on warm traffic.** Cold visitors are not ready, so they leave.
  Put a **lead magnet** in front: "a complete solution to a narrow problem" that, once solved, reveals the
  next problem your core offer solves. People who pay with time first are more likely to pay with money
  later. Same traffic, roughly 3-5x more hand-raisers (his estimate).
- **Three lead-magnet types:**
  1. **Reveal a problem** (his favourite). His B2B example is ours: a **free website speed test**. "Your site
     loads in 9 s, we take it to 3 s; every second costs about 3% of conversions." (His figure. Unverified;
     do not use it without a source.) Works best for problems that get worse while you wait.
  2. **Free trial / sample** (Costco sample): give it, then remove it, and the loss sells.
  3. **One step of many:** solve step 1 of a multi-step job (the first coat of paint, session 1 of 6).
- **Four ways to deliver:** a tool or software (a URL checker, a calculator, an assessment), information
  (guide, mini course, template), a **free service** (a free audit with some done-for-you work, only for
  qualified leads), or a physical item.
- **Free service math:** if a $250-value audit costs you $25 and 1 in 4 buys, a customer costs you $100. Fine
  if a customer is worth more.
- **Qualify before you give:** a dropdown or question routes qualified people to the valuable magnet and
  the rest elsewhere. That fixes "freebie seekers".
- **Do not solve the core problem for free.** Sell at the point of greatest deprivation (the dessert after the
  main course).
- **Ad and hook pattern: be very specific on the negatives, vaguer on the positives.** His best ad for his
  software company: "4 reasons why you'll never have a million-dollar agency". Readers think "that's me" and
  assume you can fix it. It is also more compliant than big promises.
- **"Free case study" beats a webinar for business owners:** a screen recording of "how we did X for a small
  gym" filled his calendar overnight. Sophisticated buyers smell a webinar pitch.
- **The name of the lead magnet matters more than its contents** (2-10x). Name the result, not the vehicle
  ("Big Booty Bootcamp", not "6-week deadlift seminar"). Test names with small ads, an Instagram story poll
  ("if you're not a business owner, don't answer"), texts, or "comment A or B".

### 7. Marketing & Sales Strategy for Service Based Business (PROVEN & PROFITABLE)
Channel: Adam Erhart · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1cwwF9ILYSA · 128,140 views · English · 10.4 min ·
2025-06 · 10.4 min transcribed
- Services are invisible, so the product playbook fails. You sell a promise.
- **Finish-line language:** sell life after the work, not the process. "A marketing agency doesn't sell content
  calendars; it sells steady clients and peace of mind." His health-coach example: same service, messaging
  rewritten around the result, and the calendar filled in two weeks (self-reported).
- **Feature → benefit, every time:** "3 strategy sessions" → "you'll know exactly what to focus on each week".
  Never assume the buyer connects the dots.
- **CASE funnel** (coaches, consultants, agencies, service pros, experts): traffic → opt-in freebie →
  **authority amplifier** (the thank-you page plays a short video that solves one small problem, then invites
  them to apply) → **application form** (what they struggle with and want; it makes you the one reviewing
  applications, not chasing) → calendar → a warm call.

### 8. How To Create a B2B Content Strategy that ACTUALLY Converts In 2026
Channel: Ben B2B · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QLsrenrNDe0 · 7,111 views (1.3x subs, the only outlier) ·
English · 17.4 min · 2024-09 · 17.4 min transcribed
- Chess analogy: content is not king. **Strategy is king, distribution is queen.** Random posts with no
  strategy get likes but rarely sales.
- **What blocks a B2B deal** (the opponent's pieces): macro (inflation, politics), micro (AI changing the
  industry), the client's onboarding resources, integration depth, alternatives, current contracts, the
  final decision-maker vs the purchaser. Content should knock these down one at a time.
- **Credibility in the medium itself:** a video agency proves itself with video; a CRO agency with landing
  pages. For us: show real screens of the sites and systems, not text claims.
- **Four messaging pillars:** **Angle** (where the industry is going, what you oppose: thought leadership),
  **Application** (how the service gets a result), **Advocacy** (testimonials, case studies), **Ask** (high-intent
  ads and landing pages).
- **Category entry points:** awareness without a "why would I need this now" moment does not convert. He
  knows several LinkedIn advertisers well and still never buys, because nothing triggered his need. Map
  personas → the moment that sends each one to market → the content for that moment.
- CTA and offer: decide high-touch (sales call) vs self-serve; test which offer works (free trial vs talk to
  sales).
- Distribution: intent channels (Google Ads, service-page SEO) vs demand channels (social, LinkedIn ads,
  informational SEO). Pay to amplify organic winners instead of using each format in one place only.
- Feedback loop: content engagement, CRM attribution, and **self-reported attribution** (ask every lead where
  they first saw you).
- Pick the format the person creating it enjoys; consistency follows enjoyment. Do not chase mass reach if
  your market is 100 companies.

## Instagram growth / Trial Reels

### 9. 7 New Trial Reel Ideas To Go Viral on Instagram
Channel: Build Your Tribe (Brock Johnson) · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q5QnMXkTahk · 17,088 views ·
English · 18.3 min · 2026-07 · 18.3 min transcribed
- **Eligibility (July 2026, as he relays it): at least 1,000 followers and an account older than 30 days.**
  The cap is 5-25 trials a day, varying by account.
- **Duplicate filter got stricter:** it now flags content that is merely *similar* to anything you posted,
  on the feed or as a trial, and limits its reach. Reposting a reel to the feed is still allowed and
  encouraged. Posting it again as a trial is not.
- His workaround (zoom slightly, change speed by about 5%, mirror) is filter evasion. Mirroring also breaks
  Arabic text. **Not adopted.** The legitimate version he also mentions: change real things (new hook clip,
  new on-screen text, new audio, new colours).
- **Re-trial the reels with the best followers-per-view, not the most views** (25k views and 1,000 follows
  beats 100k views and 200 follows).
- **Seven trial formats** built for strangers:
  1. Gamified: "if I reach N followers by tomorrow I'll do what the top comment says".
  2. Re-trial your best follow converters (see above).
  3. Memes on trending templates: viral, but they convert poorly to follows.
  4. **«Dear algorithm, connect me with…»**: name your exact buyer on screen. The more specific, the more
     the right people feel called out and follow.
  5. **B-roll + text**: a 1-3 sentence stat, opinion or quote over B-roll or a plain colour, or written on
     a window. Cheap to make.
  6. **First impression**: "this is a trial reel, so you've never seen me: what's your first impression /
     what am I doing wrong / guess my follower count?"
  7. "Does the algorithm prefer my polished X or me just standing here?"
- Keep trials **cheap and simple**: most trials get a few hundred to a few thousand views, so don't spend hours
  on each one.

### 10. Instagram Algorithm 2026: How It REALLY Works (New Updates Explained)
Channel: Jade Beason · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gLeeMc_Injw · 24,293 views · English · 21.2 min ·
2026-02 · 19.2 min transcribed (from 2:00)
- Pipeline: choose the algorithm (feed/stories, Explore, Reels) → gather signals, mostly about the
  *audience* → predict who will engage → rank → widen if the prediction holds. It works like matchmaking.
- **Feed and stories:** ranked mostly on your relationship with each viewer (DMs, replies, past engagement).
- **Explore:** popularity *velocity*. Likes, shares, saves and comments in the first hour, measured against
  your own normal rate.
- **Reels tab:** entertainment first; retention is the top signal; taps on your audio are a plus.
- **Not recommendable** (reach cut): low resolution, third-party watermarks (CapCut, TikTok), muted videos,
  borders, **majority-text reels** (the old paragraphs-of-text style), political topics.
- **Back-to-back posting suppresses reach.** She advises at most 1 feed post a day unless you have tens of
  thousands of followers.
- Comments: replying lifts overall engagement by about 21% (she cites Buffer research). Match the
  commenter's tone and length. A ManyChat comment-keyword CTA raises comment counts.
- **2026 change: users can choose topics ("Your Algorithm").** Rotate a fixed handful of topics (about 4)
  so you show up when someone picks that topic. Instagram sees DMs and small groups as the real engagement.

## Rules to adopt (20)

### Script
1. **Open on the buyer's role or situation in line 1** («إذا عندك مصنع…», «صاحب متجر؟», or a role
   list like ثابت's «مصمم، مونتير، مدرب… تابعني»). The same as ROAR beat 1, now confirmed by a Saudi
   source. [Thabit Hejazi, Jade "hyper-specific"]
2. **Problem-reveal hook: "N reasons you'll never…".** Be very specific about the negatives and vague about
   the positives: «٣ أسباب تخلي موقعك ما يجيب ولا طلب». The buyer thinks "that's me" and assumes we can
   fix it. [Hormozi]
3. **Add three Arabic hook types to the bank:** bold question with a threat («تعرف إنك ممكن تخسر عملاء
   بسبب هذا الغلط في موقعك؟»), statistic from a scene we saw (only numbers we actually saw or measured),
   and time-box («عطني ٣٠ ثانية وأوريك ليش…»). Test one per trial and reuse the winner. [يوتيوب سكول,
   Wael]
4. **Finish-line language + feature→benefit:** every service line names the life after («ترجع لك
   إجازة الويكند»), and every feature carries its benefit right behind it («لوحة تحكم = تعدّل أسعارك
   بنفسك بدون ما تكلم مبرمج»). [Adam Erhart]
5. **Prove it in the medium:** our reels show real screens of real client sites and systems (scrolling,
   dashboards, load-time timers), not text claims. [Ben B2B]
6. **Answer the buyer's price and risk questions as content:** «ليش الموقع بـ٥٦٠٠؟», «كم ياخذ
   وقت؟», «وش يصير لو تأخرتوا؟». The persuasion script becomes the reel. [Thabit, Sebaey]
7. **Never invent stakes or numbers** for a hook (Sebaey's fake deadline, Hormozi's "3% per second"). Only
   facts-file numbers or things we observed. [own rule, reinforced]

### Leads & funnel
8. **A cold-reach CTA is a lead magnet, not "talk to us".** Our fixed action should be a **free check**:
   «اطلب فحص مجاني لموقعك» (speed + mobile + "what's losing you orders"). It is Hormozi's reveal-a-problem
   type, and its pain grows while the owner waits. «كلّمنا على الواتساب» stays for warm and retargeting
   reels. [Hormozi, Adam Erhart]
9. **Qualify before giving the free check:** one or two questions in the WhatsApp/DM flow (sector, has a
   site?, monthly orders) route qualified owners to the free check. Others get a guide. [Hormozi, CASE]
10. **Name the lead magnet by the result and test the name** before building (story poll «إذا ما عندك
    بزنس لا تصوّت», or "comment A or B" on a trial). Example: «كم طلب يضيّعه موقعك؟» vs «فحص سرعة
    الموقع». [Hormozi]
11. **Use a "free case study" as the second magnet:** a screen recording of «كيف سوينا نظام لمصنع
    ألمنيوم في الأحساء» (real project, owner-approved facts only). Business owners trust it more than a
    webinar or a pitch. [Hormozi]
12. **Three content jobs, labelled per reel:** awareness (trend or viral, no sell; ends with a share prompt),
    consideration (free value / free check), conversion (a pure offer to people who took the free value:
    retarget them). [Wael, Ben B2B pillars]
13. **The offer must be live before posting:** bio link → a landing page or WhatsApp flow that already
    delivers the free check. Never run reach reels into an empty bio. [Wael]
14. **Ask every lead where they first saw us** (one fixed question in the WhatsApp flow), and log it.
    Self-reported attribution is the only reliable source for reels → leads. [Ben B2B]
15. **A personal face for pricing power:** the owner (Mahmoud) appears as the named expert in some
    reels. Thabit says skipping the personal brand cost him three years. [Thabit, Sebaey "personality is the
    moat"]

### Growth
16. **Trials must be genuinely different, not near-copies.** The similarity filter now catches similar
    content. Each variant changes the hook clip, on-screen text and audio, not only the first 2 s with the
    same body. Never mirror, speed-shift or zoom to dodge it. [Build Your Tribe]
17. **Re-run the best follows-per-view reels,** not the most-viewed. Add "follows per 1k reach" next to
    shares per reach in the trial scorecard. [Build Your Tribe]
18. **Trial formats for strangers:** «يا خوارزمية، وصّلني لأصحاب المصانع والمتاجر في السعودية اللي…»
    (Dear algorithm), and «أول مرة تشوفني؟ وش انطباعك عن موقعنا؟» (first impression). Both are cheap.
    [Build Your Tribe]
19. **Avoid the reach killers:** no CapCut/TikTok watermark, never muted, no borders, no paragraph-text
    reels, 1080p export. Short on-screen words are fine. [Jade]
20. **Lock about 4 rotating topic pillars** (e.g. مواقع ومتاجر / أنظمة للمصانع والشركات / تطبيقات / الذكاء
    الاصطناعي للبزنس) so the "Your Algorithm" topic choice finds us. Reply to every comment in its tone
    within the first hour, since early velocity feeds Explore. [Jade]

## Contradictions with our knowledge files

With `02-trial-reels-and-growth.md`:
1. **Eligibility is missing.** 02 lists the account constraints but not the relayed **1,000-follower and
   30-day minimum** (Build Your Tribe, July 2026). Check `fortunecodeorg` before planning the lab around
   trials.
2. **"3 hooks, only the first 2-3 s differ" may trip the duplicate filter.** 02's flow (script → 3 hooks →
   3 trials) is exactly the "similar content" Instagram now limits. Either make variants differ beyond
   the hook (rule 16), or test one hook per trial and change the body as well. Let the numbers confirm it.
3. **Volume vs one post a day.** 02 (from Brock) says posting often beats follower count, and 04 rule 25 says
   1-3 posts a day. Jade says back-to-back feed posts suppress reach, and below tens of thousands of followers
   to post at most 1 feed post a day. Reconcile: **1 feed reel a day, with the extra volume going to trials**
   (strangers only, so they don't compete in followers' feeds).
4. **Metric.** 02 says shares per reach first. Build Your Tribe judges trials on **followers per view**.
   Both are right for different jobs: shares for reach reels, follows per view for intro/trial reels, and
   leads per 1,000 reach for lead reels.
5. **Trial ads.** 02 says to boost the winner. Hormozi and Ben B2B add that paid reach should go to the
   **lead magnet** reel, not just the most-viewed reel.

With `10-script-patterns.md`:
6. **Text-led formats.** 10 says text-only reels travel when the reel delivers the value, and builds the xx
   call-and-response format on short on-screen words. Jade lists "majority-text reels" as not recommendable,
   but Build Your Tribe recommends B-roll + 1-3 sentences for trials. Reconcile: short text over moving

# @fortunecodeorg: the current Instagram identity (studied 2026-11-07, 12 latest tiles in `grid.jpg`)

The owner asked to see our own Instagram posts first, so new posts don't drift far from our identity: «علشان متروحش بعيد اوي عن هويتنا».

## What the feed looks like
- **DARK world:** near-black #121416 backgrounds, and green #00DB33 light used as a GLOW: green rim light and spill on laptops, keyboards and desks in a dark room, and a soft green radial bloom behind the subjects.
  - This is our established look on Instagram, even though the website is flat.
- **Hero objects:** a real laptop or phone, shot in a dark room with green ambient light. The screen shows a site, code, or a project (Riyadh metro, keyboard product site).
- **Type:**
  - a clean geometric Arabic sans, bold white;
  - KEY WORDS IN GREEN (e.g. «فورتشن كود», «علامتك», numbers);
  - huge green numbers for proof («800» دولار كندي، «+300 M$»);
  - centred, short headlines.
- **Story hooks on reels covers:** «العميل: أريد موقع من المستقبل», «عميل دفع $3000 / النتيجة:», «تخيل تبداء مشروعك بموقع زي كذا!!». Customer-quote and result-first hooks.
- **Brand frame on posts:**
  - the FortuneCode logo (clover + wordmark) top centre;
  - a thin rounded outline panel around the content;
  - a footer row with a globe icon «www.fortunecode.org» and a WhatsApp icon «01030608763».
- **Carousel style:**
  - a 3-tile PANORAMA (the first three posts form one wide banner: «نماذج من أعمالنا / +200 عميل», «ماذا نبني؟», «هذه هي فورتشن كود»);
  - numbered service cards with green outlines;
  - client logo strip on a white card.
- **Mascot:** a 3D black clover robot with green eyes (Clover), on an «Automation vs AI Automation» post.

## Rule for new posts and carousels
Stay inside this dark-green identity: black base, green glow, real devices, bold white/green Arabic, logo top and footer.

New craft (the collage marker marks, the tape, the kinetic ideas) may be added as ACCENTS inside it. It must not replace it with the beige paper look.

## Font (owner, 2026-11-07)
- **Static posts and carousels:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic (Bold/SemiBold headlines, Medium/Regular body).
- **Reels:** Zain mob-long.

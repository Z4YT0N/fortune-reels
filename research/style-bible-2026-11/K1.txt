FORTUNE STYLE (house look, fixed): a hand-made editorial collage. Real candid black-and-white documentary photographs (35mm Kodak Tri-X look, heavy natural grain, natural light, real Saudi people and real places) are cut out and printed as coarse halftone riso prints on warm off-white paper #F0F1F2, with torn edges, masking tape and paper fibres. A second riso ink layer in signal green #00DB33 (and deep green #006A39 for shadows) is slightly mis-registered behind the cut-outs. Over the collage, an art director has drawn by hand with a thick signal-green marker: circles, arrows, a dashed selection box with square corner handles, and a black mouse cursor. Green is used ONLY on the one object that matters in the moment plus the marker marks; everything else is black, grey and paper. Visible film grain and print noise over everything. Calm empty paper in the top quarter of the frame for typography. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. Never glossy, never CGI, never plastic skin.
Vertical 9:16.

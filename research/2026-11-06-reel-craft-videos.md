# Reel craft rules from 9 recommended YouTube videos (2026-11-06)

Source: an Instagram reel that recommended 9 videos, known only by thumbnail. Each match below was confirmed by
downloading the YouTube thumbnail and comparing it to the description; transcripts were pulled with vidIQ.
7 of 9 matched. 2 were not found and are listed at the end; nothing was guessed for them.

Each rule is written for an automated Arabic reel engine (scripted, voice-over or talking head, generated or stock

## 1. "FIX Your HOOKS" (358 -> 1.6M)

- Title: I Studied 1,000 Hooks, Here's How to ACTUALLY Go Viral
- Channel: heyDominik
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xqy9L-88wcw

Rules:
- Viewers swipe on autopilot; the target behaviour is the **scrollback**: they swipe past, the brain catches up, they come back. Build frame 1 to trigger it.
- **Context first.** A spoken line with no context ("I definitely don't cut out carbs") loses people. Put a text line at the top of frame 1 that says who/what this is about ("Lean at 45 and I eat carbs every day").
- Strongest hook type: **challenge a common belief**. Short and snappy beats a full sentence.
- **Open a loop and never close it until the next one is open.** Each payoff should open a new question.
- Do not give the answer away in frame 1. Bad: "Vanilla blueberry iced latte" (viewer already knows, leaves). Good: "This coffee should not taste good..." and **blur the giveaway object** (the blueberries) until the end.
- Respect the **safe zone**: the hook text must not overlap the caption/comment UI at the bottom or right. Hook text should be smaller than you think.
- **Declutter frame 1**: one hero subject, depth, clean background. A cluttered kitchen with overlaid text reads as noise. If the background is busy, put a solid plate behind the text.
- Text styling: vary **font weight** inside the hook (light "I eat" + medium "carbs every day") instead of all bold. Plain black-on-white / white-on-black plates work; loud red can look cheap; one accent colour + a soft outer glow on the key word is enough.
- A visual contrast in frame 1 (lean person + a big bowl of "unhealthy" carbs) strengthens a belief-challenging hook.
- Every hook must make the target viewer ask **one specific question**. "Who can relate?" asks nothing and fails.
- Story frame beats pretty pictures: "My photos 10 years ago vs now" with the person in shot creates a journey; a list of nice photos does not. Show the person and an emotion.
- Profile/feed must match the reel promise; a mismatch kills follow-through from reel traffic.

## 2. "MAKE INSTAGRAM REELS LIKE A PRO" (woman in pink blazer)

- Title: How to Make Instagram Reels Like a PRO!
- Channel: Justin Brown - Primal Video (presented by Jordana Grace)
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k4sUjTIM8fE

Mostly an app walkthrough; the reusable craft points:
- **Green-screen talking head** over a relevant image is one of her most viral formats: the background makes people ask "what is this about?"
- Green-screen staging: first few seconds the speaker **fills the frame**, then **shrinks** to reveal the background and leave room for text.
- **Change the background image often** during the reel to keep visual interest.
- **Align/match-cut transitions**: overlay the last frame of the previous clip so the next clip starts in the same position (outfit change, before/after) for a seamless cut.
- Background music sits **very low** under speech, "heard in the back of the mind".
- **Trim first, then add text, music and stickers**; never caption footage you will cut.
- Pick the **cover frame deliberately** and check the grid crop (cover is also a square/4:5 crop on the grid).
- Templates: clips of **~1-2 seconds** each, beat-synced to the track; good for low-effort filler posts.
- Use music you have rights to for cross-posting; platform-library audio is licensed only on that platform.

## 3. "0-10k Growth Hacks explained" (man with glasses, Instagram, rising chart)

- Title: The FASTEST Way To Reach 10k Followers (Instagram Expert Explains Growth Hacks)
- Channel: heyDominik
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7F7X7tfJZN0

Mostly growth strategy, not craft. Usable points:
- Reel that hides part of the payoff in the **caption** ("read the caption for real tips") keeps the reel looping while people read; this inflated watch time and helped one reel reach 218K views on an 18K account.
- Make the reel **watch-through**: put the reveal at the end.
- Data quoted (Later.com): under 1K followers, engagement rate rose from **3.1% at 1 post/week to 4.9% at 20 posts/week**. Daily reels are the way to grow; volume teaches you what the audience reacts to.
- Shares + saves + comments now weigh more than likes. Build save/share-worthy content and say "save this / send it to X" in a natural CTA.
- Copy proven formats from your niche first (pre-validated), then develop your own style.
- Stories do not grow followers; reels and carousels do. Stories convert visitors into followers.

## 4. "FIVE CREATIVE SHOTS"

- Title: 5 Creative Shots for Short Video (Instagram Reels + TikTok)
- Channel: Gaku Lange
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rbF4rEEApfM

Rules:
- Short videos (6-15 s) must tell the story fast; **varied angles** are what keep attention.
- Shot 1, **camera inside places**: inside a bag the subject reaches into, or the subject picks up the camera and carries it into the scene. Good establishing shot.
- Shot 2, **action-driven transition**: subject pushes the camera aside, or whips their head/sunglasses; pan in the same direction of the motion and cut to a new location (whip transition).
- Shot 3, **walk-and-talk backwards**: camera 3-4 m in front, walking backwards with the subject. Shows subject in motion plus environment.
- Shot 4, **height**: avoid everything at eye level. Mix eye-level, high (above subject) and ground-level-up (camera on the floor, subjects looking down into it).
- Shot 5, **POV**: camera at the eyes/mouth (bite mount) with the subject's hands in frame so the viewer feels inside the action.

## 5. "how I edit quick & aesthetic reels" (woman, pastel flat-lay)

- Title: How I make quick & aesthetic instagram reels as an artist (film, edit and post) + bonus ideas
- Channel: dearelaine
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dw1gdIW1YGs

Rules:
- Whole process ~**30 min**: 15 film, 10 edit, 5 post. Keep it repeatable.
- **Neutral, light backdrop** (light wood texture) that does not compete with the subject's colours.
- Open with the **supplies/objects + a satisfying sound** (ASMR-style tool sounds).
- Film **snippets of 30 s-1 min**, not the whole process; change angle each time so clips cut together.
- Shoot **detail close-ups from different angles** and keep the finished result a **mystery until the end**; finish with a **sweep shot** of the final result.
- Edit 16 clips (10 min of footage) down to **under 20 s**: trim each clip to its action and **speed it to ~1 second**. Prefers splicing over long time-lapses ("time-lapse isn't engaging").
- Be ruthless: if a moment does not flow, cut it.
- Pick **2-3 go-to fonts** and reuse them.
- One global grade (brightness, saturation, shadows, warmth) applied to all clips; same preset for the cover photo.
- Audio: original sound up, trending music lower; denoise in a final pass.
- Reliable formats: intro-yourself text over a clip, **3-way split-screen** collage, **reveal** (turn canvas around), sketchbook flip-through in real time, teach-one-thing, studio/space tour.

## 6. "YOUR B-ROLL SHOT LIST" (20 clip ideas)

- Title: 20 Essential B-Roll Shots Every Business Owner Needs (Repeat These Forever) | B-ROLL 101
- Channel: Natasha Pierre | Shine With Natasha
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j4mjkb3nDec

Rules (the 20 shots, grouped):
- **Content stacking** (film what you already do, 1-2x/week): walking into the office/workspace; close-up typing / working; on a call; morning ritual (coffee/matcha, becomes a brand motif); walking (0.5x back camera, feet, selfie); outfit/mirror check; planning/journaling/writing; packing a bag and leaving; behind-the-scenes detail of real work; personal hobbies/travel.
- **Content shoot** (planned, prop-led, tied to content pillars): product/prop detail shots; using the prop (writing, unboxing, organising, opening/closing, packing); reaching for an item; **walking on/off camera, walking toward camera, putting the phone down** (visual hooks); campaign-themed set (e.g. cake + streamers for a birthday sale).
- **Human tripod** (someone else holds the camera): pan, follow the subject, overhead with movement, slow push in/out, BTS of the person talking with hand gestures and emotion, walking with / behind / toward / beside the subject.
- For every action, capture **wide, close-up and overhead**: variety without new ideas.
- Quick outfit/accessory changes (hair up, jewelry, a layer) make one session look like several days.
- Camera movement must be **slow and intentional**; subject moving + camera moving fast = dizzy. Less is more.
- A recurring object (her matcha) becomes a recognisable brand cue across B-roll.

## 7. "HOW TO MAKE aesthetic content" (iced coffee, camera)

- Title: How To Create & Edit Aesthetic Videos (Instagram Reels, TikToks & Shorts)
- Channel: Modern Millie
- URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IQcPXyH71hY

Rules:
- Record **1080p / 30 fps** (platforms recompress to 1080p anyway); export settings must **match** capture settings, or the upload goes blurry.
- Turn on grid + level; **align a rule-of-thirds line to a real horizontal/vertical** (ceiling, wall corner) and keep the level indicator yellow. Nothing tilted.
- Subject either centered with symmetric lines, or on a **thirds intersection**.
- **Lower exposure** for a moody, aesthetic look.
- Shoot the same action as **wide, medium, close-up, macro, overhead, reveal and conceal**. Reveal = camera slides out from behind an object; conceal = action then camera slides behind an object.
- **Every shot must contain motion** (pouring, hand entering, steam, flickering candle). A static shot is "a photo made 3 seconds long".
- Edit: keep only the main action, **1-2 seconds per shot** (her cuts were 1-2 s); start on the establishing wide shot; **cut before the hand/motion fully leaves frame** so there is never stillness.
- Edit to the **beat** of the chosen trending track (screen-record it, extract audio, cut to rhythm, then swap in the platform audio when posting).
- One **signature filter** used on every video, intensity **<= 30%** so it looks natural; consistency makes the feed recognisable.
- HSL fix: **desaturate yellow** (and lift its lightness) so whites read true white.

## Rules to adopt (de-duplicated, max 25)

### Hook
1. **Frame 1 carries context + a question.** Top-of-frame text that says who/what (the topic) and makes the target viewer ask one specific question. Never "who can relate?" (V1)
2. **Challenge a common belief** as the default hook pattern ("X and I still do Y"), kept to one short line. (V1)
3. **Do not reveal the answer in frame 1.** Name the tension ("this should not work..."), hide or blur the giveaway object, and pay it off at the end. (V1, V5)
4. **Design for the scrollback.** One hero subject, depth, a decluttered background, and a visual contrast that matches the hook. If the background is busy, put the text on a solid plate. (V1)
5. **Hook text inside the safe zone and smaller than instinct:** clear of the bottom caption area and the right-side buttons; two font weights for rhythm; at most one accent colour. (V1)
6. **Motion from the first frame.** The hook shot must already be moving (walk-in, reach, pour, push-in). (V7, V6)

### Story / Script
7. **Loop chain:** every payoff opens the next question; close the last loop only at the end. (V1)
8. **Put the reveal last** (finished result, answer, number) so the reel is watched through; a sweep or reveal shot is the final beat. (V3, V5)
9. **Put the person and an emotion in the story** ("then vs now", a journey). A list of nice visuals is not a story. (V1)
10. **Save/share CTA spoken naturally** ("save this", "send it to someone who..."). Optionally put extra value in the caption so the reel loops while people read. (V3)
11. **Short total length:** compress the process to ~15-20 s for visual reels; real-time pacing only for "flip-through" style formats. (V5)
12. **Reuse proven formats:** belief-challenge talking head, green-screen explainer, before/after, reveal, 3-way split-screen, teach-one-thing, workspace tour. (V2, V5, V3)

### Shots / B-roll
13. **Shot variety per action:** for every beat, produce wide + medium + close-up/macro + overhead (+ reveal/conceal). Never cut eye-level to eye-level more than twice in a row; include high and ground-up angles. (V4, V6, V7)
14. **Creative angle library:** camera-inside-object (bag/drawer), walk-backwards follow at 3-4 m, POV with hands in frame, ground-up looking at subjects, overhead desk. (V4)
15. **Business B-roll bank (Arabic office context):** walk into office, typing close-up, call, coffee ritual, walking (feet / 0.5x wide), planning/writing, packing and leaving, BTS of real work, product/prop detail, reaching for an item, walking toward camera, putting the phone down. (V6)
16. **Every clip has visible motion** (hands, steam, pour, movement through frame). Reject static stock shots or animate them (slow push). (V7)
17. **Slow, intentional camera movement.** Never a fast camera on a fast subject; less is more. (V6)
18. **Green-screen staging for talking heads:** speaker full-frame for the first ~2-3 s, then shrinks to reveal a topic image that changes every beat, leaving room for text. (V2)

### Editing / Motion
19. **1-2 s per shot**, sped-up action at ~1 s for process clips; prefer splicing over time-lapse. (V5, V7)
20. **Cut before the motion leaves frame**: no dead frames at the head or tail of a clip. (V7)
21. **Cut on the beat** of the music track; the timeline is built on the track. (V7, V2 templates)
22. **Motivated transitions:** whip/push transitions follow the subject's motion direction; match cuts align the subject's position across clips. (V4, V2)
23. **One signature grade at <= 30% intensity**, applied to all clips and the cover; desaturate yellow so whites are white. (V7, V5)
24. **Audio mix:** voice/original sound up front, music low underneath ("in the back of the mind"), satisfying tool sounds kept for process shots; denoise the final mix. (V2, V5)
25. **Fixed brand kit:** 2-3 fonts max, 1080p/30 fps end-to-end (capture = export), deliberate cover frame checked against the grid crop. (V5, V7, V2)

Most software projects fail at step one: nobody defined the goal.

Here is how we run every project, and what you sign off at each step:

01 Understand: the written goal and scope
02 Quote and timeline: the contract, with scope, price and dates fixed
03 Design: the key screens, before any code
04 Build and test: each milestone, on a test link
05 Launch: the go-live date
06 Support: the support plan

No step starts until the one before it is approved. That is what keeps scope, price and timeline from drifting.

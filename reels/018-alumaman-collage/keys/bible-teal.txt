ALUM AMAN COLLAGE STYLE (fixed house look of reel 006, ink swapped to teal): a hand-made editorial collage. Real candid black-and-white documentary photographs (35mm Kodak Tri-X look, heavy natural grain, natural light, real Saudi people and real places) are cut out and printed as coarse halftone riso prints on cool light grey paper #F0F1F2, with torn edges, masking tape and paper fibres. A second riso ink layer in teal #0199a2 (and deep teal #017a82 for shadows) is slightly mis-registered behind the cut-outs. Over the collage, an art director has drawn by hand with a thick teal marker: circles, arrows, a dashed selection box with square corner handles, and a black mouse cursor. Teal is used ONLY on the one object that matters in the moment plus the marker marks; everything else is black, grey and paper. Visible film grain and print noise over everything. Calm empty paper in the top quarter of the frame for typography. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark. Never glossy, never CGI, never plastic skin, never a photoreal 3D render.
